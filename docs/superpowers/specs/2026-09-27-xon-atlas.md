# Xon-atlas redesign — Design Spec

Date: 2026-09-27
Follows: 2026-09-27-redesign-v2.md. The owner found the result busy and asked for a free-hand
redesign ("ui is ugly", "narsalar ko‘pmi… yoqimsizroq"). Direction, menu and page shape were
chosen by the owner; the boards are on the design canvas (artifact ET15MF6kb6uxUNU4NoW1nv:
Yo‘nalish, Masalalar, Masalalar — telefon, Bosh sahifa, Musobaqalar) and were approved as drawn.

## Owner's choices

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Who uses the site | Mixed: students, teachers, olympiad people |
| Colour direction | Xon-atlas |
| Menu | Compact icon rail |
| Problems page | List + one "today" row |
| Font | Keep Anthropic Sans (the canvas shows Hanken Grotesk only because it can load Google Fonts alone) |
| Dark mode | Keep, derived from Xon-atlas |

## What changed

1. **Palette.** White paper; nil `#3B30C4` for actions, links and focus; tun nili `#1F1A55`
   for the rail. Atlas colours only where they carry meaning: zumrad = easy / accepted / live,
   za’faron = medium / medal / streak, malina = hard / wrong, osmon = beginner / info. Text
   shades of zumrad and osmon are a step darker than their fills so chips pass 4.5:1. Dark
   mode is a nil night (`#110F26`), same meanings.
2. **Mark.** The ikat diamond from the direction board (nil / malina / za’faron; white on the
   rail) is the logo and favicon. The abr strip appears on the landing hero and the profile
   banner only; the girih lattice of the previous round is gone.
3. **Shell.** A dark icon rail (mark, Bosh sahifa, Masalalar, Musobaqa, Reyting, Vazifalar,
   Duellar, Urinishlar, staff's single Admin, the account menu at the foot). A slim top bar:
   the page title (or the section it belongs to), search with `/`, the streak pill. Staff
   pages get one row of tabs. Phones: a top bar with the mark and title, a dark bottom bar
   (Bosh sahifa, Masalalar, Musobaqa, Reyting, Men) and a sheet with the account, every
   destination and the theme.
4. **Calmer components.** Difficulty is a short coloured bar beside the name in ink, not a
   tinted pill. Lists are hairline rows without a box; cards are hairline boxes without
   shadow. Per-row progress bars, chips and duplicate buttons are gone.
5. **Pages as drawn.** Problems: today row, "Keyingi masala" as one line, underlined
   difficulty tabs with counts, Mavzu / Holat pickers, a plain list (status ring, number,
   title with topics, difficulty, solve rate). Home: today card with the week, rating card
   (last change, place, next tier), a one-line live contest strip, homework, submissions,
   next problems, the next contest, duels. Contests: a live hero (time left, your place and
   progress from the standings, Natijalar / Davom etish), upcoming rows with day tiles,
   finished contests by month in one grid row each (participants, your place, rating change).
   Reyting and Amaliyot ballari are two tabs of one section.
